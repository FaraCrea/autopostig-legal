Data Deletion Instructions

Queuely - Last updated October 2026

Queuely is a desktop application. It does not operate a server that stores your data: your connected accounts' access tokens, schedules, captions, comments, and messages are stored only in a local database on your own computer. The developer does not hold a copy of this data.

Delete your data

  1. Open Queuely, go to Connected Accounts, and choose Disconnect next to the account. This immediately deletes the stored access token and that account's posts, comments, and messages from your computer.
  2. To remove everything, uninstall Queuely and delete its data folder (%APPDATA%\autopostig on Windows).

Revoke Queuely's access on the platform

  • Instagram: Instagram app, Settings and privacy, Website permissions (Apps and websites), then remove the Queuely app.
  • Threads: Threads app, Settings, Account, Website permissions, then remove the Queuely app.
  • TikTok: TikTok app, Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services, then remove Queuely.
  • Facebook Page: Facebook, Settings, Business integrations, then remove the Queuely app.
