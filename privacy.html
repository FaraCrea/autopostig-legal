Privacy Policy

AutopostIG - Last updated September 2026

AutopostIG is a single-user desktop app. It does not run on a server and does not sell or share your data with third parties.

1. What is stored, and where

Post captions, schedules, and media files are stored in a local database on your own computer. Instagram, Threads, and TikTok access tokens are encrypted and stored locally only.

2. What AutopostIG does with platform data

AutopostIG uses the official Instagram, Threads, and TikTok APIs, authorized by you, to publish the posts you schedule and to read comments and messages on your own connected accounts. None of this data is sent to any server operated by the developer.

3. Media staging

Immediately before publishing, media files are temporarily uploaded to an object storage bucket you configure yourself, since platforms require a public URL to fetch media from. Files are removed after the post is published.

4. Your control

  • Disconnect any account at any time to delete its stored access token.
  • Export or delete your local database and media at any time from within the app.

5. Changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
