Privacy Policy

AutopostIG - Last updated October 2026

AutopostIG is a desktop scheduling tool for content creators and small businesses. Each user connects accounts they own or are authorized to manage, and schedules and publishes posts to Instagram, Threads, TikTok, and Facebook Pages. It does not run on a server operated by the developer, does not sell your data, and does not use your data for advertising.

1. What is stored, and where

Post captions, schedules, and media files are stored in a local database on your own computer. Access tokens for connected accounts (Instagram, Threads, TikTok, Facebook Page) are encrypted and stored locally on your computer only.

2. TikTok data and permissions

When you connect a TikTok account, AutopostIG requests only the permissions needed to work:

  • user.info.basic - your TikTok display name, avatar, and account ID, shown inside the app so you can tell which account is connected.
  • video.upload and video.publish - to send or publish the videos and photos that you created and scheduled to the TikTok account you connected, via the TikTok Content Posting API. Before each post you choose the privacy level, interaction settings and commercial content disclosure, and confirm the post.

Content is posted only when you schedule or request it. AutopostIG does not read your TikTok followers, messages, or other users' data, does not sell or share TikTok data with third parties, and does not use TikTok data for advertising or profiling. TikTok data is used only to provide the posting feature described above.

3. Other platforms

For Instagram, Threads, and Facebook Pages, AutopostIG uses each platform's official API, authorized by you, to publish the posts you schedule and to read comments and messages on your own connected accounts. This data stays on your computer.

4. Services you configure yourself

Some optional features send data to services that you set up and control with your own accounts and keys. The developer does not receive this data.

  • Object storage (e.g. Cloudflare R2): immediately before publishing, media files are temporarily uploaded to a bucket you configure, because platforms require a public URL to fetch media. Files are removed after the post is published.
  • Companion mobile app (Firebase): if you enable it, schedule information and captions are synced to your own Firebase project so you can view them on your phone.
  • AI caption assistant: if you use it, the text you ask it to work on is sent to the AI provider whose API key you entered (for example Anthropic, Google Gemini, or Groq), under that provider's own terms.

5. Your control: revoking access and deleting data

  • Disconnect any account inside the app at any time; this deletes its stored access token from your computer.
  • You can also revoke AutopostIG's access directly on each platform. For TikTok: TikTok app, Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services (or Manage app permissions), then remove AutopostIG.
  • You can export or delete your local database and media at any time from within the app, or by uninstalling it and deleting its data folder.
  • To request help with data deletion, email the contact address below.
