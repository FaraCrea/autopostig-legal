Plan your content once. Publish everywhere you create.

AutopostIG is a desktop scheduling tool for content creators, social media managers and small businesses. Write a caption, pick your media, choose the accounts and the time, and AutopostIG publishes for you through each platform's official API.

Features

Multi-platform scheduling

One composer for Instagram, Threads, TikTok and Facebook Pages, with fields specific to each platform.

Calendar and queue

See everything planned on a calendar, reorder the queue, reschedule or pause a post at any time.

Multiple accounts

Connect several accounts per platform and choose which ones each post goes to.

Mobile companion

Optional companion app to review your schedule and get notified when a post goes out.

Caption assistant

Optional AI assistant using your own provider key to draft and refine captions.

You stay in control

Nothing is sent to a platform until you create and approve a post. Disconnect any account in one click.

How it works

  1. Connect your account with the platform's official login. AutopostIG never sees your password.
  2. Create a post: choose media, write the caption, pick the accounts and set visibility options.
  3. Review the options and confirm. Schedule it for a date and time, or publish it right away.
  4. AutopostIG sends the post at the scheduled time and shows the result in your queue and calendar.

TikTok integration

AutopostIG uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API so creators can publish their own videos and photos to their own TikTok account.

  • user.info.basic shows which TikTok account is connected (display name and avatar).
  • video.upload sends a created video or photo to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  • video.publish publishes the post directly after the creator has chosen the privacy level, interaction settings and commercial content disclosure, and has agreed to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation.

Before scheduling, the creator always chooses who can view the post, which interactions to allow, whether the post promotes a brand, and confirms the post. Posts carry no watermark or promotional text added by AutopostIG.

Who it is for

Creators and small businesses that publish regularly and want to prepare content in advance. Each user connects only accounts they own or are authorized to manage.
